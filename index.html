QUICK ANSWERS

How far ahead should I book?

Quick bakes need 3 days. Celebration cakes need at least 5 days, bespoke 3 to 4 weeks, and weddings 3 to 12 months. Check your date at the top of the page to see straight away.

Do you deliver?

Yes, up to 5 miles from Harpenden. It's £6 within Harpenden and £10 up to 5 miles. Collection from Harpenden is free.

How does the deposit work?

For custom cakes, a 30% deposit secures your date and the rest is due a week before collection. Quick bakes are paid in full when you order.

Can I change my design?

Of course, up to 7 days before collection. After that the ingredients are bought and the decorating is underway.

What if I need to cancel?

Let me know as soon as you can. Custom cake deposits are refundable up to 14 days before your date. Quick bakes can be cancelled free up to 48 hours before.
